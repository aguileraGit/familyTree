This page walks through the core things you'll do most often: adding people, editing them, and organizing them into family groups. The last section explains what the colors on each person's circle actually mean.

1. Adding People

New people are added from the Add New Person card in the left sidebar.

  1. Fill in First Name, Last Name, and Sex — these three are required.
  2. Optionally choose the person's Father and/or Mother from the dropdowns, if they're already in the tree. Leave either as "None" if that parent isn't tracked (yet).
  3. Optionally choose an Immediate Family Group — the household this person primarily belongs to. Pick an existing group, leave it as "None," or choose + Add New Family Group to create one on the spot (see Section 3).
  4. Click Additional personal information to expand optional fields: middle name, nickname, other family groups they're also a member of, birthday, place of birth, and current location.
  5. Click Add Person. They'll appear on the tree immediately.
Screenshot: the "Add New Person" card, expanded to show the Additional personal information section

2. Editing People

  1. Click on a person's circle on the tree. This opens the Person Details panel below the tree, showing their name, family group badges, and how many siblings, aunts, uncles, and cousins they have.
  2. Click the Edit button in that panel.
  3. The sidebar form switches into edit mode — the header changes to "Edit Person," the fields fill in with that person's current details, and the button changes to Update Person. A Cancel button also appears if you want to back out without saving.
  4. Make your changes and click Update Person.
Dragging a person to reposition them on the canvas saves automatically — you don't need to open Edit for that. Edit is only for changing their details (name, parents, groups, etc.).
Screenshot: the Person Details panel with the Edit button, and the sidebar form in "Edit Person" mode

3. Creating & Managing Family Groups

A family group is just a named, colored label — like "Hinson Family" or "Aguilera Family" — that you can assign to people. There are two ways to create one:

A) While adding or editing a person

  1. Open the Immediate Family Group dropdown and choose + Add New Family Group.
  2. In the modal, type a name and pick a color from the swatches shown.
  3. Click Create & Select — the new group is created and immediately assigned to the person you're adding or editing.
Screenshot: the "Add New Family Group" modal with the name field and color swatches

B) From Manage Groups

  1. Click Manage Groups in the View Options card.
  2. To add a group: type a name in the "New group name" field, pick a color from the swatches below it, and click Add.
  3. To rename a group: edit its name field and click the checkmark button.
  4. To recolor a group: click the small colored circle next to its name — a color palette opens beneath it. Pick a new color and it saves immediately.
  5. To delete a group: click the trash icon. This removes the group label but does not delete any people.
Screenshot: the "Manage Groups" modal, ideally with one group's color palette expanded

4. Immediate Family Group vs. Also a Member Of

Every person can belong to family groups in two ways:

  • Immediate Family Group — the one primary household this person belongs to. This is what draws the colored border box around a cluster of related people on the canvas (when "Show family borders" is turned on).
  • Also Member Of — any additional groups this person belongs to, for example someone who married into another family and is tracked in both. There's no limit to how many of these a person can have.

What the ring around a circle means

The outer ring color is always about sex, not family group — blue for male, pink for female. This is separate from everything below.

What the fill color(s) inside a circle mean

The inside of the circle shows family group membership:

  • Solid color — the person belongs to exactly one group: their Immediate Family Group.
  • Pie slices — the person belongs to more than one group. The slice starting at the top and going clockwise is always their Immediate Family Group; the remaining slices are the groups listed under Also Member Of, in the order they were added.
  • Solid dark gray — the person isn't assigned to any family group at all.
The colored border boxes drawn around clusters of people only follow the Immediate Family Group. "Also Member Of" groups show up as extra color slices on a person's circle, but that person won't get a second border box for them.
